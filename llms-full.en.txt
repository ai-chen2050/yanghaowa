## 3. Features in detail

### 3.1 Three threads that unfold by age band
- **Summary**: After you add a child, the health, emotional-growth and money-sense content appears for their current age band: no pocket money at age one, no brushing check-ins for a teenager.
- **Detail**: Content is organised into 0-1, 1-3, 3-6, 6-12 and 12-18 bands, each with main tasks, a stage theme and the panels that belong to that stage. One stage map gates everything, so when a child crosses into a new band the whole app changes together. Content is compiled from Chinese paediatric and developmental-psychology standards, with sources noted.

### 3.2 Log today in one tap, not as a chore
- **Summary**: Time outdoors, screen time, the family activity, brushing and today's mood are one tap on the home card; height, expenses, reminders and wish goals each open a form in a single step.
- **Detail**: The card only tracks what actually changes day to day, so there is no completion pressure. Every entry can be undone in place and saves without a confirmation dialog. Logs feed the growth overview, stage reports and the simulator's reference dots, so the app looks more like your family the more you log.

### 3.3 Personality growth simulator
- **Summary**: Drag six parenting sliders and a nine-axis personality radar updates in the same frame. Sliders start from your own parenting-style quiz, and a set of "what the family practises" dots from your logs sits beside the radar.
- **Detail**: The model is a trend simulation grounded in developmental-psychology research, computed locally with linear weights; it is not a prediction for any individual child, and the page says so. Reference dots appear only where a dimension has at least three data points, describe the parents' effort rather than grading the child, and no score is ever shown. You can save setups to compare, or ask the in-app assistant to comment on the current one.

### 3.4 Wenwen: AI on your own key
- **Summary**: Wenwen is the small companion in the corner. It asks about today's mood, surfaces reminders and discusses parenting questions with you. You configure the model key, and you preview what is sent before it goes.
- **Detail**: This is the app's only network activity. By default it sends the age band plus trend summaries within the scopes you allow; never names, birthdays or health details, and money data is off by default. Each scope has its own switch, and a card lists every item actually injected before the request. In regions requiring algorithm registration, the AI entry is disabled at the channel level.

### 3.5 Reminders and family challenges
- **Summary**: Vaccines are scheduled from the birthday, water and medicine reminders ring on time, and challenges like eye care, early bedtime and daily praise can run side by side for the whole family.
- **Detail**: All reminders are scheduled on the device; there is no push server. Water and medicine use exact alarms, the rest ring by the day, and tapping a notification lands on the matching panel. Checking in on a challenge means ticking an age-appropriate checklist, not a single box.

### 3.6 Your data: on-device, backed up, portable
- **Summary**: No account, no server. A local snapshot is saved daily; export encrypted backup files; on iOS keep a copy in your own iCloud; merge on import so a child is never duplicated.
- **Detail**: Backups are readable JSON, optionally passphrase-encrypted. Import offers merge or overwrite; merge matches a child by name plus birthday, and the preview counts come from the same code that writes. A snapshot is taken before any overwrite and can be undone within 24 hours. AI conversations and the most private self-care notes never enter an export.

## 4. FAQ

### What is Yanghaowa?
A local-first parenting companion for parents of children aged 0 to 18. It follows three threads, physical health, emotional growth and money sense, by age band, from profile to age-staged content and logs, stage reports and AI discussion. Available on iOS and Android.

### How is my data protected?
There is no account and no server of ours. Every record lives in a local database on your phone, and the app contains no analytics or ad SDK. The only network call is the AI request made with your own key, which by default sends the age band and trend summaries, with a card listing each item before it goes. Backup files are yours to keep, optionally passphrase-encrypted.

### Is it paid? How does pricing work?
The download is free and the basics work: profiles, age-staged content, one-tap logging and reminders. Advanced features are unlocked by Pro, a one-time purchase that lasts for life, with no subscription and no ads. Topic skill packs are small separate one-time purchases that make the assistant sharper on that topic; they are not part of Pro and do not include model costs. Prices are shown in your local store, and "Restore purchases" brings everything back on a new phone.

### What does the AI need, and what does it send?
You enter an API key from a model provider in the app; usage is billed between you and that provider. Requests include only the child's age band and trend summaries within scopes you allow, such as growth percentile ranges, habit check-in days and task completion rates; never names, birthdays or exact measurements, and money data is off by default. Each scope has its own switch, and a card lists every injected item before sending.

### Is the personality simulator accurate?
It is a trend simulation built on developmental-psychology findings, not a precise prediction for any child, and the page says so. The six sliders start from your own parenting-style quiz, and the reference dots come from your real logs, but those describe what the family practises rather than scoring the child. Treat it as a thinking tool that turns abstract parenting ideas into visible cause and effect.

### How do I move my data to a new phone?
Export a backup from "Me → Data & backup" (the encrypted version needs a passphrase), send the file to the new phone and choose "Restore from backup → Choose a file" on the same page there. On iOS with iCloud private backup on, a new device on the same Apple ID can pick "Backup in iCloud" directly. Import offers merge or overwrite; merge never duplicates a child, and a snapshot is taken before overwrite so you can undo within 24 hours.

### What about more than one child?
You can add several children and switch by flicking left or right on the home and growth pages. Each child's panels follow their own age band, and logs, reminders and reports are kept separately.

### Does it replace a doctor or counsellor?
No. Health content follows Chinese paediatric standards but is a record-keeping and reference aid only; for growth concerns, vaccine schedules or emotional and behavioural issues, follow your doctor and qualified professionals. The relevant screens carry disclaimers.
