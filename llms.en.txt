# Grow Together (yanghaowa, 养好娃 App, Grow Together Parenting, Yanghaowa)

> Grow Together is a local-first parenting companion for parents of children aged 0 to 18. It tracks and guides three threads by age band: physical health, emotional growth and money sense, closing the loop from profile to age-staged content and logs, stage reports and AI discussion. No account, no server: every family record stays on your own phone, and AI runs on a model key you bring, with a preview before anything is sent. Core features are free; Pro is a one-time purchase that unlocks everything for life.

## Core Features

1. **Three threads that unfold by age band**: After you add a child, the health, emotional-growth and money-sense content appears for their current age band: no pocket money at age one, no brushing check-ins for a teenager.
2. **Log today in one tap, not as a chore**: Time outdoors, screen time, the family activity, brushing and today's mood are one tap on the home card; height, expenses, reminders and wish goals each open a form in a single step.
3. **Personality growth simulator**: Drag six parenting sliders and a nine-axis personality radar updates in the same frame. Sliders start from your own parenting-style quiz, and a set of "what the family practises" dots from your logs sits beside the radar.
4. **Wenwen: AI on your own key**: Wenwen is the small companion in the corner. It asks about today's mood, surfaces reminders and discusses parenting questions with you. You configure the model key, and you preview what is sent before it goes.
5. **Reminders and family challenges**: Vaccines are scheduled from the birthday, water and medicine reminders ring on time, and challenges like eye care, early bedtime and daily praise can run side by side for the whole family.
6. **Your data: on-device, backed up, portable**: No account, no server. A local snapshot is saved daily; export encrypted backup files; on iOS keep a copy in your own iCloud; merge on import so a child is never duplicated.
