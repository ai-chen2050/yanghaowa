Privacy Policy

Effective date: 2026-09-06 · Applies to Raisewell for iOS and Android

This is the Huawei AppGallery edition: that build has no cloud-connected analysis features, and no data ever leaves your device.

Raisewell is a fully on-device parenting and growth companion. There is no account system and we run no servers of our own. Below we spell out four things plainly: what we collect, where it is stored, whether it ever leaves your device, and how you delete it.

1. What we collect

We collect no personal information. The app has no sign-up or login, does not read device identifiers, location or contacts, contains no analytics or advertising SDKs, sends no usage data and has no crash reporting. Everything you enter in the app — child profiles, height and weight, vaccines, vision, teeth, outdoor and screen time, moods, tasks and challenges, money accounts and expenses, photos and notes — lives only on your device. We cannot see any of it.

2. Where data is stored

All records are kept in a local database (SQLite) inside the app's private directory; memory-wall photos are copied into the app's own documents folder and the originals are left untouched. Any AI service key you configure yourself is kept in the system's secure storage (iOS Keychain / Android Keystore). On the first launch of each day the app writes a backup snapshot on the device; exported backup files are saved wherever you choose. On iOS, if you turn on "iCloud private backup", backup files are placed in this app's documents container under your own iCloud account and synced by Apple's iCloud between devices signed in to the same Apple ID; we have no way to access that container.

3. Third-party services

The app integrates no third-party analytics, advertising, push or account services. Purchases are completed through the Apple App Store, Google Play or Huawei AppGallery; the transaction is handled by that store under its own privacy policy, and all we receive is the single fact of whether a purchase exists — never your payment details.

5. Deleting data

At any time you can clear AI conversations or delete all data under "Me → Data & backup → Danger zone"; deleting all data also removes the on-device snapshots. Uninstalling the app deletes all data on the device. Backup files you exported elsewhere or placed in your own iCloud are yours to delete. Because we hold no data, there is no "ask us to delete it" step.

6. Children's privacy

This app is intended for parents and primary caregivers, not for direct use by children, and it collects no information from children. The records you enter about your child stay on your device under your full control. Please do not type your child's name, school or other identifying details into AI conversations; the app will not send them on your behalf by default.

7. Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, we will update this page and the effective date above; material changes to how data is handled will also be announced inside the app.
